Privacy

What we collect: nothing.

Last updated June 2025. The whole policy on one page.

The short version

Cowboy Quick Shot doesn't collect personal information. Motion data, game scores, and your settings stay on your device. We don't have servers, we don't have analytics, we don't sell anything.

What we don't collect

  • Names, email addresses, or contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Camera or microphone access (we never request these)
  • Browsing or app usage history
  • Motion data outside the app session

What stays on your device

  • Your reaction time scores and personal bests
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Tournament history (pass-and-play mode)

This data is stored in standard iOS app storage and is removed if you delete the app.

Third parties

We don't integrate any third-party analytics, ad networks, social SDKs, or tracking tools. The only "third party" involved is Apple, which handles App Store transactions and crash reports per Apple's own privacy policies.

Children

The app is safe for all ages. Since we don't collect data from anyone, there's nothing to specifically protect for children under 13 — it's already not collected.

Changes

If we ever change this policy, we'll post the new version here with an updated date. If we ever start collecting any data (we don't plan to), we'll require explicit opt-in.
